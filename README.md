# websites-demo

Concept landing pages and small websites, each one turning a business idea into a polished, professional site.

This repo has two goals:

1. **Show the work:** quality, professional sites that look right on a phone.
2. **Show the method:** sites that don't look AI-generated, because they're built from a clear spec and design rules, not by repeatedly re-prompting an AI site builder.

> **Concept projects:** all businesses shown here are fictional. Each is based on a real kind of business, with a made-up name and details.

## How these are made

Every site starts on paper, not in a prompt:

- **A written spec** for the whole project: [`docs/superpowers/specs/`](docs/superpowers/specs/)
- **Shared design rules** every site must meet (mobile, accessibility, honest calls to action): [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md)
- **A brief per site** (`BRIEF.md`) describing the business, its audience and what makes it different
- **Design notes per site** (`DESIGN.md`) explaining every visual choice, and why it fits that brief

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com), deployed on [Vercel](https://vercel.com). The work is AI-assisted with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code), working from the documents above. The project rules for that are in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).
