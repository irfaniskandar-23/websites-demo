# Solidvent: brief

> Concept project. Solidvent is a fictional business, based on a real kind of business in Malaysia: aircond servicing.

## The business

**Solidvent** services air-conditioners for homes and small businesses across **Penang Island**: general servicing, chemical washes, leak checks, gas top-ups and repairs.

## Problem

Penang households struggle to find an aircond service they can rely on.

1. **Small service areas.** Many technicians only cover one part of the island, for example George Town but not Bayan Baru or Bayan Lepas. People in the south struggle to get anyone to come.
2. **Informal bookings.** Appointments are made over WhatsApp chat. There is no confirmed slot, no booking proof and no invoice afterwards, so customers can't hold anyone to anything.
3. **No track record.** There are no reviews or records of past work to look at, so customers are trusting a stranger with a job inside their home.
4. **Price surprises.** The quote says RM60, then on the day it becomes "gas kurang, tambah RM150". Aircond gas doesn't run out unless there is a leak, so many top-ups aren't needed at all.

## Audience

- **Primary: households** in condos and landed homes across Penang Island, usually with 1 to 4 wall-mounted units. They care about a fair price, trusting the person inside their home, and a tidy job.
- **Secondary: small businesses** such as offices, shops and restaurants. They care about proper invoices for their accounts, regular servicing on a schedule, and work done outside opening hours. Restaurant units clog faster from kitchen grease and need servicing every 2 to 3 months.

## Edge over competitors

Each point answers one of the problems above.

1. **The whole island.** Solidvent covers every area of Penang Island, not just one district. Areas served: George Town, Jelutong, Air Itam, Gelugor, Tanjung Tokong, Tanjung Bungah, Batu Ferringhi, Bayan Baru, Bayan Lepas, Sungai Ara, Teluk Kumbar and Balik Pulau. Seberang Perai is not covered.
2. **Proper bookings, not chat.** Customers book a confirmed slot, get a reminder before the visit, and can reschedule or cancel themselves. After the job, the invoice arrives by email, plus a reminder when the next service is due.
3. **A record of every job.** After every visit the customer gets a **service report** with before and after readings, what was done and the technician's notes. All work carries a **30-day warranty**. Solidvent builds trust with records the customer can check, not with claims.
4. **Prices known before any work starts.** Standard jobs have fixed, published prices. Inspection is free, with no call-out fee. Repairs get a written quote first, and the customer can say no and pay nothing. Gas is only topped up after a leak check.

### What a service report contains

| Check | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Air temperature at the vent | 19°C | 13°C |
| Filter | Clogged | Washed |
| Drain pipe | Slow, dripping | Clear |
| Gas pressure (R32) | 130 psi, normal | No top-up needed |
| Warranty | | Valid for 30 days from the service date |

## Pricing

| | Wall unit 1.0–1.5hp | Wall unit 2.0–2.5hp | Cassette or ceiling unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General service** (filter wash, coil spray, drain flush) | RM70 | RM90 | RM150 |
| **Chemical wash** (unit taken apart and deep-cleaned) | RM170 | RM200 | RM330 |

- **Whole-home bundle:** 3 or more units in the same visit, RM10 off each unit.
- **Inspection:** free, with no call-out fee. Repairs are quoted in writing before any work.
- **Gas top-up:** only after a leak check. From RM120, depending on the gas type, quoted in writing.
- **Business maintenance plan:** 4 services a year at RM60 per unit per visit, with after-hours slots and one monthly invoice.

These prices are in line with real Penang and Malaysian rates. They are not the cheapest, but they are honest.

## Personality

An **organised, straight-talking engineer**.

- Explains the *why* in plain words: "Gas pressure normal, no top-up needed."
- Calm and precise. Never salesy: no "best in Penang", no exclamation marks, no urgency tricks.
- Shows the numbers instead of making claims.
- A light local touch in small places, such as area names or a "terima kasih" at the end of a report, so it never feels cold.

## Site scope

**A single landing page.** Everything the business needs to say fits on one scroll: what Solidvent does, the prices, how booking works, the service report and warranty, the areas covered, the business plan and common questions. A multi-page site would split thin content across pages and take 3 to 5 times the work.
